3. Operational Logs

Application operational logs exclude personal content, including submitted text, images, filenames, appointment details and client IP addresses. Hosting-provider infrastructure logs are separate from these application logs and may include connection information. Operators of self-hosted copies can disable application logging completely; they control their own hosting and retention settings.
